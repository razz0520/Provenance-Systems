The Maharashtra Food & Drug Administration (FDA) suspends food licences of seven Mumbai establishments, including McDonald's in Colaba, Bombay Presidency Radio Club, Novaara in Malad, Status Restaurant at Nariman Point and Khar Gymkhana, citing serious food-safety and hygiene violations. Separately, three Mumbai establishments were sealed and six people arrested in a gutkha/pan masala crackdown, with prohibited products worth 1.95 lakh seized. The Maharashtra Food & Drug Administration (FDA) conducted a statewide special inspection drive on August 19-20 targeting milk and dairy products, prohibited gutkha/pan masala, and other food products.
